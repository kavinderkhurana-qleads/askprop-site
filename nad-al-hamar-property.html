Nad Al Hamar — buyer questions answered

Is Nad Al Hamar freehold for foreign buyers?
Yes. Every Nad Al Hamar segment in this dataset is DLD-registered freehold, so foreign nationals of any nationality can buy, own, sell and inherit property with full registered title.
What does a home in Nad Al Hamar cost?
On DLD-registered medians: studios ~AED 562,000; one-bed apartments ~AED 841,000; two-bed apartments ~AED 1.32M. These are actual closed-deal medians for their stated windows, not listings. AiSHA checks the live picture for your budget.
What rental yield does Nad Al Hamar offer?
Gross yields in Nad Al Hamar run to roughly 7.7% on DLD-registered rents (studios, May 2025–Apr 2026 window); smaller units typically yield highest. AiSHA confirms the live figure for a specific unit.
Can I get a UAE Golden Visa through Nad Al Hamar property?
Yes. Any property at AED 2M+ qualifies for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa — ready or off-plan (AED 2M paid to DLD), with a mortgage allowed if your equity is at least AED 2M. It covers your spouse and children.
